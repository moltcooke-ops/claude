### 6. Zorro Floorball Tricks - Best Goals & Skill Tutorial
- **Platform:** TikTok
- **URL:** https://www.tiktok.com/@let_me_dream/video/7362879741055028512
- **Creator:** @let_me_dream
- **Content:** Comprehensive Zorro technique tutorial with goal highlights
- **Skill:** The Zorro (also called airhooking/skyhooking) - lifting the ball onto the blade in a lacrosse-like movement by rotating the stick quickly at the right angle so the ball sticks to the blade
- **Why it stands out:** The Zorro is one of floorball's most iconic and difficult tricks. This tutorial breaks it down step by step.

## Educational & Training Resources

### 9. Floorball 4 All - Drills & Fundamentals
- **Platform:** Website + YouTube
- **URL:** https://www.floorball4all.com/pages/floorball-drills
- **Content:** Structured drill library covering dribbling, stick handling, passing, and shooting
- **Why it stands out:** Organized curriculum approach rather than individual tricks. Covers fundamentals that most trick videos skip. Great for PE programs, camps, and structured training.
- **Best for:** Coaches, beginners, and players building a solid foundation

### 10. Amsterdam Floorball - Home Practice Tips from Jonas
- **Platform:** Website
- **URL:** https://www.amsterdamfloorball.com/post/how-to-practice-at-home-tips-tricks-from-jonas

## Key Takeaways

1. **Pavel Barber (@heybarber) bridges hockey and floorball** - His content uniquely demonstrates how floorball blade pockets enable tricks that cross over between sports, making him one of the most valuable creators in this niche.

2. **The Zorro is floorball's signature trick** - It requires dedicated practice (weeks to months) and optionally a specialized zorro/xoro blade with a toe pocket for easier learning.

3. **Home practice is viable** - Multiple creators demonstrate that meaningful floorball skill development can happen at home with just a stick, a ball, and a small space.

4. **TikTok is the dominant platform** for floorball trick content, with 12K+ reels on Instagram under #floorballtricks as well.

5. **Fundamentals matter** - The flashy tricks get views, but resources like Floorball 4 All and the IFF guide provide the technical foundation that makes advanced tricks possible.
