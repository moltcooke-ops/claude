# Product Marketing Context

## Organization
**Hockey Growth Initiative** — a non-profit dedicated to getting floor hockey gear into the hands of every school-age kid in British Columbia.

## Mission
Make floor hockey accessible to every student in BC by providing free equipment and programming to schools across the province.

## What We Do
- Donate floor hockey equipment (sticks, balls, instructional guides) to schools
- Support floor hockey programming in BC public schools
- Run community events and hockey experiences for kids
- Partner with schools, educators, and communities to grow the game

## Key Differentiator
The only non-profit supported by the Canucks for Kids Fund (CFKF) that hands out floor hockey gear to every school in BC. No other organization does this at our scale.

## Proof Points
- **700 schools** receiving equipment across BC
- **350,000 kids** playing floor hockey through our programs
- Backed by the **Canucks for Kids Fund (CFKF)**
- Province-wide reach: every region of British Columbia
- Event photos and community engagement documented on Instagram @hockeygrowthinitiative

## Target Audiences

### 1. Parents of School-Age Kids
- Want their kids active, safe, and having fun
- Care about accessibility (floor hockey doesn't require ice time or expensive gear)
- Respond to seeing their kids and schools featured
- Motivated by community, inclusion, and opportunity

### 2. Hockey Players & Hockey Community
- Love growing the game at the grassroots level
- Care about accessibility and removing barriers to entry
- Want to see hockey culture thrive beyond the rink
- Respond to stories of impact and kids discovering the sport

### 3. Executives, Board Members & Donors
- Need clear impact metrics (schools reached, kids served)
- Want to see ROI on donations and partnerships
- Care about brand alignment, media coverage, and community goodwill
- Respond to professional reporting, event recaps, and growth data

### 4. Schools & Educators
- Need free, easy-to-implement programming
- Want equipment that's safe and age-appropriate
- Value curriculum-aligned resources and instructional guides
- Motivated by student engagement and physical education outcomes

## Brand Voice & Tone
**Source:** Instagram @hockeygrowthinitiative

- **Community-first**: We spotlight the kids, the schools, the volunteers, and the communities — not ourselves
- **Energetic & positive**: We celebrate every win, every school, every kid who picks up a stick
- **Authentic & real**: Event photos, behind-the-scenes, candid moments over polished graphics
- **Inclusive**: Floor hockey is for everyone — every skill level, every background, every part of BC
- **Proud but humble**: We share our impact with gratitude, not boasting
- **Conversational**: Friendly, approachable, like talking to a neighbor at a community event
- **Action-oriented**: "Sign up," "Get involved," "Tag your school," "Share this"

### Voice Do's
- Use "kids" not "students" or "youth" (unless formal context)
- Show real photos of real events
- Celebrate schools and communities by name
- Keep it short and punchy for social
- Use hockey language naturally ("stick," "game," "play")
- Tag partners and schools

### Voice Don'ts
- Don't use corporate jargon or nonprofit-speak ("stakeholder engagement," "capacity building")
- Don't be overly formal or stiff
- Don't make it about the organization — make it about the kids
- Don't use stock photos when real photos exist
- Don't oversell — let the impact speak for itself

## Language
English (Canadian)

## Key Messages
1. **Every kid deserves a chance to play.** Floor hockey removes the barriers of ice time, expensive gear, and travel.
2. **700 schools. 350,000 kids. Every corner of BC.** This is the largest floor hockey equipment program in the province.
3. **Backed by the Canucks for Kids Fund.** The only non-profit of its kind with CFKF support.
4. **Floor hockey is for everyone.** Safe, inclusive, and fun for all skill levels and ages.
5. **We don't just hand out gear — we grow the game.** Equipment, programming, events, and community.
